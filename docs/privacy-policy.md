# Voice Calendar Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

Voice Calendar is a personal calendar assistant for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

Voice Calendar does not collect, store or transmit your personal data to the developer or to any third party. Everything you create in the app stays on your device or in the Apple services you already use.

## Information the app uses

- **Microphone and speech recognition.** When you tap the microphone button, or when the app is open and listening for the wake phrase, audio from the microphone is sent to Apple's Speech framework to be turned into text. Where your device supports it, recognition happens on the device. Otherwise Apple may process the audio on its servers under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer never receives audio or transcripts.
- **Calendar and Reminders.** Events and reminders you create are written to the calendars and reminder lists on your iPhone through Apple's EventKit framework, so they sync with the Calendar and Reminders apps and your iCloud or other calendar accounts. The developer has no access to this data.
- **Notes and tasks.** Notes and tasks are stored only on your device.
- **Notifications.** The app schedules local notifications on your device for upcoming events, reminders and tasks. No push notification service is used.
- **Preferences.** Settings such as your wake phrase and accent colour are stored on your device.

## What the app does not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No data is sent to servers operated by the developer.

## Your choices

You can withdraw any permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. Deleting the app removes its notes, tasks and preferences; calendar events and reminders remain in Apple's apps unless you delete them there.

## Children

Voice Calendar is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
